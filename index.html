Our story

Grilled by a couple who never stopped tending the coals.

MiChie-Ken started as a weekend lechon manok stand run out of a home kitchen in Pasig. What kept people coming back wasn't a secret spice — it was the coals, tended by hand, and a rule the couple behind it never broke: never skimp on flavor to save on price.

Today the same fire cooks every whole chicken, slab of liempo, and crispy pata that leaves our two branches, alongside a grilled bangus lineup for the days you want something lighter but just as smoky.

  • Cooked to orderNothing sits under a heat lamp — it comes off the grill when you order it.
  • Two ways to get itWalk in for pick-up or have it delivered to Manggahan and Maybunga.
  • Built for groupsBundle promos pair chicken, pata and liempo for the whole table.
  • Priced pang masaReal portions without the fine-dining markup.
